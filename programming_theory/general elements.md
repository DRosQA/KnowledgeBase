## duck typing
"If it looks like a duck and quacks like a duck, it's a duck".

idea that if a class has a method, you can invoke said method, regardless of what type you're calling it on. Used in languages without strong typing / with dynamic typing.

ex. Pizza(MenuItem).get_price() and ToiletAccess(Services).get_price() are duck typed
